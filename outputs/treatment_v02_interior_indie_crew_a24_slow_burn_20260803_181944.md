# THE AMBER GRID

## 1. FILM TITLE & LOGLINE
- **Working Title:** The Amber Grid
- **Genre Blend:** Psychological Drama / Single Room Thriller
- **Logline:** During a sudden citywide blackout, an anxious electrician is visited by an estranged childhood friend carrying a dangerous secret and eyeing an unopened letter on the counter.

## 3. NARRATIVE SYNOPSIS & THEMATIC ARC
### Act I: Setup
In a dim Seattle apartment, electrician Leo examines an unopened letter sitting on his kitchen counter just as the city power grid fails, plunging the building into darkness. A heavy knock at the door reveals Julian, Leo's long-lost friend whom he hasn't seen in seven years, seeking shelter from the pouring rain.

### Act II: Escalation
As the two navigate an uncomfortable reunion illuminated only by a battery-operated flickering table lamp, subtle hostility emerges regarding past betrayals. The tension is briefly interrupted when Arthur, the eccentric upstairs neighbor, barges in holding an empty vintage coffee mug, making bizarre observations about the darkness before wandering off.

### Act III: Climax & Resolution
Left alone again, Leo catches Julian attempting to steal the unopened letter from the counter. In a quiet, high-stakes emotional confrontation, Julian reveals the truth about the letter's contents before walking out into the pitch-black hallway, leaving Leo alone with his choice.

### Thematic Arc & Core Motivation
The film explores how buried guilt resurfaces in moments of vulnerability, using the physical blackout to reflect the characters' internal moral darkness.
