# THE DARK PHASE

## 1. FILM TITLE & LOGLINE
- **Working Title:** The Dark Phase
- **Genre Blend:** Psychological Drama / Single Room Movie
- **Logline:** During a sudden citywide power outage, an anxious man is forced by his eccentric neighbor to confront an unopened letter that holds the key to his past.

## 2. CHARACTER ROSTER & CASTING

| Barnaby Vance | 40s, eccentric posture, loud voice, wearing oversized vintage cardigan. | Supporting | No |

## 3. NARRATIVE SYNOPSIS & THEMATIC ARC
### Act I: Setup
During a blackout in a Seattle apartment, Arthur fixates nervously on an unopened letter resting on his kitchen counter. Interrupting his solitude, his eccentric upstairs neighbor Barnaby bursts in seeking shelter from the dark.

### Act II: Escalation
As the apartment grows colder and pitch dark, conversation shifts from mundane humor to lingering personal secrets. Barnaby notices the letter and challenges Arthur about what he is desperately avoiding.

### Act III: Climax & Resolution
Pushed to a breaking point, Arthur opens the letter, exposing a painful truth about a past mistake. The high-stakes quiet confrontation ends in quiet acceptance just as the lights flicker back on.

### Thematic Arc & Core Motivation
Overcoming paralysis caused by guilt and isolation through unexpected human connection during darkness.
