# SUNBURN AND STAPLERS

## 1. FILM TITLE & LOGLINE
- **Working Title:** Sunburn and Staplers
- **Genre Blend:** Fish Out of Water / Vacation Film
- **Logline:** When an overprepared college student loses her wallet at a lavish Las Vegas pool resort, she must use a heavy-duty desk stapler and quirky ingenuity to settle her bill before her dream holiday turns into a nightmare.

## 3. NARRATIVE SYNOPSIS & THEMATIC ARC
### Act I: Setup
Sally, a dedicated college student taking her very first vacation in sunny Las Vegas, finds herself stranded at an exclusive resort pool cabana. After ordering high-end refreshments, she realizes her wallet is missing, leaving her with an unpaid tab and an intimidating cabana host named Vic demanding payment.

### Act II: Escalation
Refusing to panic, Sally searches her oversized study backpack and finds only academic textbooks and a bright red heavy-duty stapler. Noticing nearby vacationers struggling with broken sandals, ripped entry wristbands, and fluttering event flyers, Sally steps out of her comfort zone and offers rapid, hilarious stapler-based quick fixes in exchange for cash tips.

### Act III: Climax & Resolution
After fixing a promoter's stack of ruined VIP passes with precision stapling, Sally earns enough cash to pay Vic in full. She staples her paid receipt directly to Vic's ledger, finally relaxing and embracing the chaotic, unpredictable fun of Las Vegas.

### Thematic Arc & Core Motivation
Overcoming rigid academic perfectionism to embrace adaptability, resourcefulness, and spontaneous real-world enjoyment.
